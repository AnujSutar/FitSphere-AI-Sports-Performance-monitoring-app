## Brand & Style
The design system embodies "Kinetic Daylight"—an athletic, high-performance sports-tech aesthetic engineered for daylight clarity and kinetic momentum. It targets data-driven athletes, coaches, and sports science enthusiasts who require precision metrics without cognitive fatigue. 

The aesthetic is built on a crisp Minimalist foundation infused with technical sports-performance cues: pure quartz whites, slate structure, razor-sharp typography, and explosive kinetic accents. The emotional resonance is focused, invigorating, and decisively premium—evoking the brisk atmosphere of an early-morning track workout paired with laboratory-grade biometric intelligence.

## Colors
The palette balances ultra-clean luminous backgrounds with high-density charcoal typography and bio-kinetic energy accents.

- **Primary (`#16a34a`):** Kinetic Emerald. Used for high-priority performance indicators, primary interactive drivers, active recording states, and progress milestones.
- **Secondary (`#0d9488`):** Electric Teal. Governs biometric secondary loops, AI telemetry insights, oxygenation indexes, and technical analysis tags.
- **Tertiary (`#0ea5e9`):** Velocity Cyan. Reserved for real-time telemetry pulses, sensory feedback, chart vectors, and dynamic system state counters.
- **Neutral (`#0f172a`):** Deep Obsidian Slate. Establishes uncompromised readability for headlines, metrics values, and structural framing.

Surface tiers transition from pure white (`#ffffff`) for elevated interactive cards, down to crisp off-white quartz (`#f8fafc`) for base canvas backgrounds, flanked by structural borders in refined slate (`#e2e8f0` and `#f1f5f9`).

## Typography
Typography reflects an athletic-scientific balance:
- **Headlines & Metric Numerals:** Space Grotesk offers geometric acceleration, technical tightness, and an athletic kinetic edge. Use negative tracking to preserve structural density.
- **Body & Longform:** Hanken Grotesk provides unhindered neutral legibility across intensive sports metrics, coaching logs, and dashboard streams.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Space Mono is implemented for biometric telemetry, timestamps, zones, targets, and system status markers, standardizing character alignments across high-frequency visual readouts.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-grid architecture configured across a 12-column foundation for desktop (`>1024px`), an 8-column layout for tablet (`768px-1023px`), and a 4-column system for mobile (`<767px`). 

Spacing maintains tight internal card density for telemetry while preserving generous breathing room between visual modules. Outer canvas gutters ensure sports analytics dashboards retain breathing room on widescreen displays. All vertical section rhythms are strictly derived from the 4px baseline grid to ensure pixel-level alignment of modular data blocks.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated primarily through tonal layering and low-contrast perimeter micro-borders rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Base Layer:** Slate Quartz (`#f8fafc`).
- **Surface Layer (Cards, Modules):** Pure Snow (`#ffffff`) bounded by a hairline 1px border of `#e2e8f0`.
- **Active Elevation (Floating Overlays, Flyouts):** Soft daylight ambient occlusion: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Kinetic Accent Glow:** For high-alert or PR-breaking stats, a faint kinetic tint shadow can be applied: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.18)`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses `roundedness: 1` (Soft), yielding subtle 4px (`0.25rem`) baseline radii for small interactive components, 8px (`0.5rem`) for cards and inputs, and 12px (`0.75rem`) for key focal containers. This keeps the aesthetic aerodynamic, disciplined, and technical, steering clear of toy-like or pill-shaped consumer tropes in favor of an elite sports instrument feel.

## Components

### Primary & Action Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Kinetic Emerald (`#16a34a`) background, `#ffffff` text, font `label-lg`, uppercase, with 8px radius (`rounded-lg`). Hover shifts to `#15803d` with an active micro-scale transition of `98%`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#e2e8f0` border, deep slate text (`#0f172a`). Hover introduces `#f1f5f9` surface fill.
- **Telemetry Action:** Subdued Velocity Cyan tint (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.08)`) with `#0284c7` typography and border.

### Chips & Stat Badges
- Compact height (24px to 28px), rendered in Space Mono (`label-md`). 
- Features a 4px corner radius (`rounded-sm`), subtle tinted backgrounds (e.g., emerald at 10% opacity: `rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.1)` with `#15803d` text), paired with a 1px border of matching tone. Used for cadence zones, recovery indexes, and strain indicators.

### Cards & Analytical Widgets
- Pure `#ffffff` background, enclosed in a 1px `#e2e8f0` border.
- Headers are paired with a subtle hairline divider (`#f1f5f9`). Data values utilize `metric-numeral` in Deep Obsidian (`#0f172a`), accompanied by mono-spaced delta trackers (+2.4% in `#16a34a`).

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Inputs:** 40px height, `#f8fafc` background with a 1px `#cbd5e1` resting border. Focus transitions instantly to a crisp 1.5px `#16a34a` kinetic stroke with a faint focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.15)`. Text in Hanken Grotesk `body-md`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px square or circular bounds in `#ffffff` with 1.5px `#94a3b8` border. Checked state fills with `#16a34a` displaying a pure white check icon.

### Biometric Telemetry Bars & Sparklines
- Base tracks use `#f1f5f9`. Progress indicators utilize solid Kinetic Emerald (`#16a34a`) or Electric Teal (`#0d9488`) fills without skeuomorphic gradients, highlighting precise targets cleanly against the daylight backdrop.
